# Lending Portfolio & Credit Risk Analytics

## Project Overview

This project analyzes a retail lending portfolio containing 10,000 issued loans to evaluate portfolio performance, credit risk exposure, borrower characteristics, repayment behavior, and collections efficiency.

The project simulates how Business Intelligence and Analytics teams within banking and financial services organizations monitor loan portfolios, identify risk drivers, track repayment performance, and support lending decisions.

---

## Business Objectives

- Assess overall portfolio health and performance.
- Identify high-risk borrower segments.
- Analyze delinquency and repayment behavior.
- Evaluate loan portfolio exposure across customer groups.
- Monitor collections and recovery performance.
- Build executive-level dashboards for lending analytics.

---

## Dataset Information

Source: Lending Club Loan Portfolio Dataset

Records: 10,000 Loans

Domain: Retail Lending / Consumer Finance
